## The claim
Henley Bike Repairs wants you to believe their prices are simple and fair, and to bring your bike in.

Mostly none. It's a plain price list. Two things matter:

Also noticed: "Most jobs done the same day; we'll ring you if it's going to be longer" is a soft promise, not a guarantee. It's ordinary shop talk.

## Flip it
"Full service: $180 *minimum*, final price depends on what your bike needs."

## Neutral numbers
- Standard to full is $180 − $95 = **$85 extra** for wheel truing and bearing regreasing.
- The puncture price of $25 includes the tube, so there's no hidden add-on.
- The "parts extra" cost can't be calculated, because no parts prices are given.
- The "parts extra" wording appears on the full-service line only. The standard service line doesn't say whether parts are extra, though gears and brakes adjustments could also need parts.

## Questions to ask them
1. Does "parts extra" also apply to the standard service, for example brake pads or cables?
2. What's the labour rate if something extra turns up mid-job?
3. Will you get my approval before any parts are fitted? "Quoted before we start" suggests so, but ask them to confirm.
4. If the job runs past the same day, is there any storage or delay charge?

## Verdict: **Fair**
The only catch is that the full service price is a floor, not a total. The text says so itself and promises a quote before work starts. Nothing key is hidden.

**Action:** Book the service you need, and ask question 1 about parts when you do.
