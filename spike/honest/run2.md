- Full: $180 + parts. The parts amount isn't given, so I can't work out a total.
- The full service costs $85 more than standard ($180 − $95) for wheel truing and bearing regreasing. Whether that's worth it depends on whether your wheels or bearings actually need it.

## Questions to ask them
1. Does "parts extra" also apply to the standard service, for example brake pads or a chain?
2. What do common parts usually cost on a full service (cables, pads, chain)?
3. Is the quote written, and can I decline after hearing it? Is there a charge for that?
4. What's the "longer" timeframe when a job can't be done the same day, and is there a storage fee?
5. Can you tell me which service my bike needs before I book?
