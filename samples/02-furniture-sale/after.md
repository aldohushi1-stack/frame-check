## The claim
Buy this lounge now, on finance, because it's a $2,500 saving that ends soon.

## Frames spotted

| Frame | Their words | Flip |
|---|---|---|
| Anchor | "Was $3,999… Save $2,500… over 60% off" | Was it ever sold at $3,999, and for how long? The maths is right (2,500 ÷ 3,999 = 62.5%), but the "was" price is the claim to test. |
| Small unit | "Just $15 a week" | The total over the full term, including fees (below). |
| Omission | "0% interest" in the headline, fees in the asterisk | 0% interest isn't 0% cost. |
| Urgency / scarcity | "4 days only", "Only 6 left", "Sale ends Sunday" | If you wait, you may pay more or lose this exact lounge. You can also look at other lounges in the meantime. |
| Social proof | "Australia's #1… over 50,000 families" | #1 by what measure, and who checked? |

## Questions to ask them
1. What was the sale price history? How many were sold at $3,999 in the last 6 months?
2. What does the lounge cost with no finance? Is there a cash discount?
3. If I don't clear the balance by month 24, is interest charged on the whole original amount or only on what's left?
4. Are there early-repayment or late-payment fees on top of the $9.95 monthly fee?
5. What is "#1" based on, and what are the delivery, warranty and return terms on leather?

## Verdict: Pushy but true
The numbers are disclosed and the arithmetic holds, so I wouldn't call it misleading. The pressure comes from the framing: the high anchor, the weekly figure, and the deadline. It would tip into misleading if the lounge was never really sold at $3,999.

**Next step:** compare similar leather lounges at about $1,500 elsewhere, ask question 1, and buy with cash or a card you'll clear early. Skip the account and monthly fees. The 4-day deadline shouldn't make the decision for you.
